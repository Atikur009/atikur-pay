How verification works

No bKash API key. No merchant account. No waiting.
An Android app on your phone reads the incoming bKash SMS (amount, sender and TrxID) and forwards it to Atikur Pay. When your customer enters the TrxID at checkout, we match it against the SMS — same TrxID, same amount, same sender — and complete the payment. Your phone must stay on with internet for verification to work.
Fees. Atikur Pay charges no extra fee. bKash's own charges apply as per your bKash account.
