SMS auto-verification setup

Install the Atikur Pay SMS app on the phone that has your bKash SIM. The app reads incoming bKash SMS and forwards them to this server, so TrxIDs are verified automatically. Your phone must stay on with internet.

SMS endpoint: POST /api/sms

Headers: X-SMS-Secret: <your SMS ingest secret> and X-API-Key: <your API key>

Body (JSON):

{
  "sender": "01712345678",
  "amount": 128.55,
  "trx_id": "9HX7K2M4P1",
  "sms_text": "You have received Tk 128.55 from 01712345678...",
  "method": "bkash"
}
